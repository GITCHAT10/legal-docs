# Red Flag Registry

Any item below blocks advancement until a named human owner resolves and documents it.

## Data

- Unknown, unlicensed or restricted data provenance
- Missing point-in-time history
- Suspected look-ahead, label leakage or survivorship bias
- Unverifiable alternative-data collection method
- Personal or confidential data without lawful basis and access controls

## Research integrity

- Failed trials omitted from the experiment ledger
- Benchmark, window or metric changed after results were observed
- Sharpe improvement without economic rationale
- Excessive parameter sensitivity
- Candidate selected from an unbounded search without multiple-testing correction
- Result cannot be independently reproduced

## Market and portfolio risk

- Liquidity assumptions exceed observable capacity
- Material short borrow, financing, FX or settlement assumptions omitted
- Concentration, drawdown or expected-shortfall limit breached
- Performance dominated by one position, asset, venue or regime
- No credible unwind or rollback plan

## Technology and cyber

- Generated code requests production credentials
- Dependency is unpinned, untrusted or materially vulnerable
- Research environment has unrestricted network egress
- Agent attempts to edit guardrails, approval records or audit history
- Unsigned artifact or mismatched checksum
- Hidden executable, obfuscated logic or unexplained binary

## Legal and regulatory

- Activity may constitute regulated advice, dealing, arranging, custody, exchange operation or securities issuance without written review
- Tokenized asset lacks jurisdiction-specific legal opinion, issuer authority, title analysis or investor-eligibility process
- Sanctions, AML/KYC, beneficial-ownership, tax or privacy assessment is incomplete
- Marketing language implies guaranteed returns, guaranteed compliance or legal finality

## Governance

- Human approver is also the sole strategy author or validator
- Fewer than two authorised human approvals
- Approval does not identify exact commit and evidence bundle
- Approval is expired, conditional requirements are unmet, or rollback ownership is missing
