# BLACKCORAL PRIME — AutoResearch Governance Pack

Status: **Governance specification only**  
Authority: Founder, Board, formally appointed directors and authorised human signatories  
Execution posture: **Advisory-only, research-only, no capital deployment**

This folder defines the legal, risk and model-governance boundaries for a future AutoResearch environment. It does not contain trading code, exchange connectivity, wallet keys, smart-contract deployment logic or an automated execution path.

## Core rule

A research agent may propose an experiment. It may never approve, merge, deploy, trade, transfer capital, alter risk policy or sign a transaction.

## Required lifecycle

1. Registered research hypothesis
2. Data-rights and provenance review
3. Isolated experiment
4. Walk-forward and stress validation
5. Independent model-risk review
6. Investment Committee paper
7. Human approval or rejection
8. Separate, authorised implementation process
9. Post-approval monitoring and kill-switch readiness
